## swatkats.info

**[swatkats.info](https://swatkats.info/)** is the fan encyclopedia this game leans on, and we are
very grateful to its editors and contributors. We use it for:

- **Show reference:** characters, villains, episodes, the TurboKat and its weapons (every special
  missile in the game, https://swatkats.info/entry-type/swat-kats-weapons/).
- **Character portraits and comic cutscene panels:** show frames from the character entries
  (`tools/fetch_character_art.py`).
- **Missile card pictures** on the cockpit weapons screen, from the missile catalogue
  (`tools/fetch_missile_art.py`).
- **Music:** "Big Trouble" (launcher) and "Drop Bomb" (Episode 1 story) from the SWAT Kats score,
  from the site's music library (music.swatkats.info). Both are owned by their rights holders and
  are used here only in a non-commercial fan project.
