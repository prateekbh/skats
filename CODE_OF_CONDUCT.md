# Code of Conduct

This is a small fan project made for fun. Be kind: treat everyone with respect, assume good
intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and keep discussion welcoming to
newcomers of every experience level. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not
tolerated. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and
may ban repeat offenders.

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
